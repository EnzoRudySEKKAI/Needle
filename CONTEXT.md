# Needle Domain Language

## Structure

The complete ontology map, presented as a tower, campus, or cruise ship. A structure contains one or more ordered floors.

## Floor

A spatial slice or deck of the structure. Every concept belongs to exactly one floor. Floor navigation is personal view state and is not synchronized between collaborators.

## Neighborhood

A semantic grouping of concepts. A neighborhood may span several floors and has an independently positioned flag on each floor where it appears.

## Structure View

An architectural overview of the complete structure. Its interactive cutaway previews one simplified floor at a time; editing remains in the focused floor view.

## Relation

A directed connection between two concepts. A relation is either full, drawn as a continuous path, or dotted, drawn as a visibly broken path.
