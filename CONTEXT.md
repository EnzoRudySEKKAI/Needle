# Needle Domain Language

## Building

The complete ontology map. A building contains one or more ordered floors.

## Floor

A spatial slice of the building. Every concept belongs to exactly one floor. Floor navigation is personal view state and is not synchronized between collaborators.

## Neighborhood

A semantic grouping of concepts. A neighborhood may span several floors and has an independently positioned flag on each floor where it appears.

## Building View

An exploded overview of every floor. It supports exploration and selection; editing remains in the focused floor view.
